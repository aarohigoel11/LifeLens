## 🧠 What is LifeLens?

**LifeLens is an AI-powered decision intelligence system** that helps individuals and organizations make **high-impact decisions using predictive modeling and personal behavioral insights.**

> ❌ GenAI gives answers  
> ✅ LifeLens predicts outcomes  

---

## ❗ The Problem

We live in an age of unlimited information — yet people struggle with clarity.

📊 From initial research (108 respondents):  
- **67%** feel stuck or uncertain every month  
- **58%** are willing to pay for better decision-making tools  
- **60%** trust a personalized digital version of themselves :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}  

👉 The gap is clear:  
> People don’t lack information.  
> **They lack clarity about what they should do.**

---

## 💡 The Solution

LifeLens introduces a new category: **Decision Intelligence**

Instead of generic advice, it:
- builds a **personal cognitive model**
- learns your behavior, goals, and constraints
- runs **simulations on your future decisions**
- gives **predictive insights, not suggestions**

---

## ⚙️ How LifeLens Works

### 🔗 1. Data Integration Layer
Connects with apps using APIs (OAuth):
- Google Calendar 📅  
- Notion / Todoist 📝  
- LinkedIn 💼  
- Health & Finance Apps 📊  

---

### 🧬 2. Personal Knowledge Graph (PKG)
Transforms your life into structured intelligence:

- **Entities:** goals, habits, tasks, skills  
- **Attributes:** energy, duration, patterns  
- **Relationships:** actions → outcomes  

👉 This becomes your **digital version of you**

---

### 🔮 3. Simulation Engine (Core USP)

Runs predictive models like:
- productivity forecasting  
- burnout risk prediction  
- career trajectory modeling  
- habit success probability  

Example outputs:
- 📈 +23% productivity improvement  
- ⚠️ 30% lower burnout risk  
- ⏳ Goal completion 3 weeks faster  

---

### 🤖 4. Decision Intelligence Layer

Final output:
- personalized predictions  
- future impact reports  
- decision confidence scores  

---

## ✨ Example: GPT vs LifeLens

### ❌ GPT:

### ✅ LifeLens:

👉 GPT gives advice.
